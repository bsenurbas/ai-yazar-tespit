The case of the ivory cufflink came to us on a frost-bound morning, when Baker Street wore that hard, metallic silence which follows a night of snow. Holmes had breakfasted upon coffee alone and was standing by the window, tracing some pattern upon the pane with his forefinger, when a heavy step sounded upon the stair. “A countryman,” said he. “A man of means, though careless in dress, and recently bereaved.” The door opened, and a broad-shouldered gentleman entered, clutching a small velvet box. “Mr. Holmes, my brother has vanished from our house in Surrey, and the police have found only this.” Holmes took the box and lifted out a single ivory cufflink, carved with the head of a fox. “My dear sir,” said he, “your brother has not vanished. He has been invited.”

“Invited?” cried the visitor. “By whom?” Holmes turned the cufflink in the light. “By someone who knew his vanity better than his habits. See here, Watson: no mud in the carving, though the ground outside was wet. It was not dropped in the garden, as the police suppose. It was placed there. The ivory is warm-toned and old, yet the clasp is new. A sentimental object repaired in haste.” He looked sharply at our guest. “Your brother hunted, dressed well, quarrelled over money, and received a letter yesterday afternoon.” The man’s mouth fell open. “He did. From a lady in London.” “No,” said Holmes. “From a man who used a lady’s paper.”

By dusk we were at the Surrey house, where Holmes examined the garden path, the library grate, and the silver tray near the hall stand. “The matter is not obscure,” said he at last. “Your brother left willingly, but not knowingly. He believed he was going to a private meeting; instead he walked into a trap laid by a debtor who feared exposure.” He pointed to a pale smear upon the tray. “The ink from the letter was not dry when it was placed here, yet the post had arrived three hours earlier. Therefore it was written inside the house.” He turned toward the silent secretary, whose face had lost all colour. “You repaired the cufflink, wrote the note, and led him to the north gate. I suggest you tell us where he is, before the snow makes your silence permanent.”
